# Roadmap

Seven stages. Each one ends with something you can open and look at. Hours are rough estimates.

## 1. Bubbles alive (about 30 hours)

- [x] Project setup
- [x] A bubble whose bounce depends on its mood
- [ ] Bubble module with tests
- [x] A real map of Soho from OpenStreetMap, with a converter for other areas
- [x] Bubbles that pick somewhere to go and travel along streets
- [x] A population of wild bubbles living on the map
- [x] Day and night that follow real London time and the real sunset, with bubbles going out after dark and sleeping

## 2. Characters (about 35 hours)

- [ ] The cast types: Diva, Worrier, Schemer, Romantic, Old-Timer, Chaos One, Foodie
- [ ] Needs that rise and fall: hunger, tiredness, loneliness, boredom
- [ ] Whims, and actions built from mix-and-match parts
- [ ] Meetings: bubbles that like each other stick together, others bounce apart
- [ ] Journals written from what actually happened

## 3. Your Pip (about 20 hours)

- [ ] Drop one bubble and mark it as yours
- [ ] Tap any bubble to follow it and read its journal
- [ ] Saving on the player's device, with a backup code
- [ ] A coverage meter for how much of the area has been explored

## 4. Dreams (about 40 hours)

- [ ] The dream: a short bounce level, one a night
- [ ] Points worth pocket money, and rare treasures worth much more
- [ ] Wake up and keep it, or keep dreaming and risk it
- [ ] Wealth shown as size, with a cap
- [ ] Spending decided by the bubble's character

## 5. Shared map (about 40 hours)

- [ ] A small guest-book server that stores each bubble's record
- [ ] Every browser working out the same bubbles from those records
- [ ] Everyone's bubbles on one map

## 6. Slang and stories (about 40 hours)

- [ ] Made-up words that spread, compete and drift
- [ ] A dictionary that fills itself in as you watch
- [ ] Chain reactions between bubbles
- [ ] An evening recap of the day's best moments

## 7. More areas (about 40 hours)

- [ ] Chinatown and Covent Garden, unlocking as Soho is explored
- [ ] Bubbles travelling between areas through stations

## Rules the game keeps

- No AI inside the game
- No sign-in, and no text typed by players
- Play money only
- Real businesses are never named or judged
- Nobody can control a bubble
