# Roadmap

The game started as bubbles on a real map of Soho. It is now one dot on a made-up island, built around its journal. The Soho version is still in the git history.

## 1. The island and the journal (done)

- [x] An island grown from a seed, hidden under chart-paper fog that clears as the dot explores
- [x] Paths worn where the dot walks
- [x] Ten places to find, each with its own first line and some with follow-ups
- [x] Two neighbours who go from strangers to named to friends
- [x] Gifts left by tapping the map
- [x] Days and nights, sleeping at home, and the dot naming itself and building a roof
- [x] A journal you can tap to jump to places or filter by neighbour
- [x] Pause, speed up, and skip to tomorrow
- [x] Phone layout, with the journal under the map

## 2. Bringing back the best of the earlier versions (done)

- [x] A bounce and face that show each dot's mood
- [x] Characters for the neighbours: Odo the Worrier and Bim the Diva
- [x] Made-up words in speech bubbles, and a word list that fills itself in
- [x] Drifting clouds, golden dusk and a softer night, with a lit window at home
- [x] Drag, pinch, scroll and double-tap zoom, with a button to find Pip again
- [x] The dream: a short level once a night, with treasures kept at home

## 3. A toy to play with (done)

- [x] A toybox: berry, ball, hat, umbrella, kite and flower, each used differently by each character
- [x] Neighbours take the toy they love off your dot once they know it
- [x] Poke any dot to make it jiggle and complain
- [x] Rain sends everyone for cover; wind blows hats off and lifts kites
- [x] Flowers that grow over two days and cheer up anyone nearby

## 4. The town in the north (done)

- [x] A causeway that appears once most of the island is found
- [x] A town grown from a seed: crooked streets, a square, cafés, a bakery, a pub, a theatre, shops and a market
- [x] The rest of the cast living there: Schemer, Romantic, Old-Timer, Chaos One, Foodie
- [x] Coins from dreams, spent by character, with richer bubbles growing bigger
- [x] Windows lit after dark
- [x] Tap anyone to read their journal
- [x] A meter for how much of the town is explored

## 5. Bouncing inside the strange places

Tap a found place and the view tips sideways into a short bounce level inside it. Roll, jump, pop word bubbles, mind the thorns. Each one answers a bit of the mystery.

- [x] The Stairless Tower: bounce up the inside and read the writing at the top
- [x] Air: knocks deflate you, air bubbles pump you up, bigger bounces higher
- [x] Every dot has its own course: poke it, then tap "Play?"
- [ ] The Humming Cave: dark tunnels, the hum gets louder near the way out
- [ ] The Door in the Hill: down the stairs past the tally marks, deeper each visit
- [ ] The Old Bridge, Lookback Pond and the Stone Ring
- [ ] Squash small and puff up big; floating in water

## 6. Keep it going

- [ ] Save on the player's device, so the island and journal are still there tomorrow
- [ ] Tests for the story rules
- [ ] More follow-up lines, so places stay interesting after the first few visits

## 7. The mystery

- [ ] Answer, slowly, where everyone went: the tally marks, the stones, the old ones who went north
- [ ] Things the dot finds and keeps at home
- [ ] Places that change over the days

## Ideas from the first plan, not decided yet

- Other islands

## Rules the game keeps

- No AI inside the game
- No sign-in, and no text typed by players
- Nobody can control the dot
